What it covers

Weeks 1–4, and only those

Everything on the midterm was taught in the first four weeks. Each page below is the reference for its week — re-read it, re-run its widgets, and answer its check-yourself questions without looking.

Week 1
UML & Object-Oriented Programming
The four pillars, abstract base classes, and reading and drawing class diagrams.
Week 2
Concurrency & Async in Python
The event loop, tasks and blocking calls; threads, processes and the GIL.
Week 3
Socket Streaming & Protocols
OSI layers, TCP vs UDP, framing, FIX / ITCH / OUCH and the arena’s WebSocket contract.
Week 4
Backtesting and Its Data
What a backtest is for, where tick data lives, logical vs real time, and an honest backtester.
How to prepare

Revise what you built, not something new

  • Re-do each week’s check-yourself questions on its page, then explain every answer in one sentence.
  • Re-read your own code from labs and homework 1–4: be able to say why each class, task, message and clock is there.
  • Redraw one diagram per week from memory — a class diagram, an event-loop timeline, a message exchange, a backtest event loop — and compare it with the page.
  • Bring questions to Ed before the exam; logistics (time, room, rules) are announced in your course’s LMS.
